Everything on your farm

A whole little world to tend

From the first seed to a bustling production chain — grow at your own relaxing pace.

Plant & harvest

Sow pumpkins, sugar cane and more on your planting beds, then harvest them once they ripen.

Orchards & bushes

Grow apple, cherry, orange and peach trees plus berry bushes that yield fruit again and again.

Raise animals

Keep chickens and cows in their pens and collect the goods they produce for your kitchen.

Craft in factories

Juicers, churns, ovens and more turn raw goods into tasty products through multi-step recipes.

Fulfill orders

The task desk asks for goods you can make — deliver them for coins and reinvest in your farm.

Wheel of Fortune

Spin the daily wheel for a little bonus and keep your farm growing every single day.

The satisfying loop

Every harvest feeds your factories, every delivery funds your next big upgrade.
